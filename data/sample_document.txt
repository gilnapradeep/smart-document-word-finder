Artificial Intelligence is a branch of computer science
that focuses on creating intelligent machines.

Machine Learning is a subset of Artificial Intelligence.
It allows computers to learn from data and improve their
performance without being explicitly programmed.

Deep Learning is a part of Machine Learning.
It uses neural networks to understand complex patterns
and process large amounts of information.

Natural Language Processing helps computers understand
human language. It is used in chatbots, translation,
text classification and sentiment analysis.

Computer Vision enables machines to understand images
and videos. It is used in image recognition, object
detection and medical image analysis.

Machine Learning algorithms use training data to build
models. These models make predictions based on patterns
identified in the data.

Neural networks consist of interconnected layers.
They are widely used in Deep Learning applications.
